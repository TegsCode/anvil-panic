## ANVIL PANIC — classic build

Junk falls. You move. Score is however long you last.

No levels, no saves, no grind — you die, you press space, you are playing again
in under a second.

### Downloads

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)** | `AnvilPanicClassic.exe` — one file, no installer, double-click |
| **Android 8.0+** | `AnvilPanicClassic.apk` — one file, allow "install unknown apps" |

Both files are self-contained: the game, the art and the audio are inside the
file. Nothing else to download, no folder of assets, no runtime.

The `.exe` is not code-signed, so Windows will show *"Windows protected your PC"*
once. **More info → Run anyway.** The APK is self-signed, which is normal for
anything that does not ship through Google Play.

### Controls

**Desktop** — `←` `→` / `A` `D` move · `Shift` dash · mouse or touch to steer ·
`Space` restart · `M` mute · `F11` fullscreen.

**Android** — landscape only, three input modes switchable in the top-right:

- **BTN** on-screen left/right pads, dash in the middle
- **TILT** lean the phone; calibration is automatic on start
- **TOUCH** tap and hold to steer there, swipe to drag, double-tap to dash

### What this build is

The **classic** version: one cream blob, one taunt, three donuts for a shield,
heavy junk that starts hopping around 22 seconds in. No characters, no powers, no
voice lines. Kept exactly as it was.

The version with six characters and powers is in development — there are
screenshots of it on the [download page](https://tegscode.github.io/anvil-panic/).
None of it is in this download.

### Privacy

No account, no ads, no network. The Android build does not even request the
`INTERNET` permission. Your best score is stored on your own device, next to the
game.
